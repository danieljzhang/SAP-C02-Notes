## How to Use

1. Start with the domain matching your weakest objective.
2. For each uncertain choice, read the linked service notes and verify current capabilities, quotas, pricing, and availability.
3. Practice explaining why the closest alternative does not satisfy the stated constraint.
4. Revisit missed scenarios by objective, not just by service name.

> Exam facts and domain weights can change. Verify them against the [official SAP-C02 exam page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-professional/).
